Terms & Conditions

Circular By Terra — User Agreement

Welcome to Circular By Terra. By accessing our platform, scheduling pickups, dropping off waste, or logging in as a partner or collector, you agree to these Terms & Conditions.

1. Waste Segregation & Safety

Users agree to segregate recyclable and biodegradable materials properly. Hazardous materials, bio-medical waste, and contraband are strictly prohibited.

2. Fair Weight & Pricing

Weight measurements are recorded on certified digital scales at collection or gate verification. Commercial scrap rates are indicative and finalized based on quality and dry weight.

3. Green Points & Rewards

Green Points earned represent environmental contribution and certified CO₂ emission reductions. Points hold no cash value and are non-transferable.

4. Collector & Partner Code of Conduct

All collectors (kabariwalas), recycling units, and households must treat each other with respect and maintain transparent handoffs verified by QR codes.

5. Scrap Payout & Verification Schedule

When the weight is confirmed at the doorstep by the authorized kabadiwala, the household is paid 20% of the actual amount. The remaining 20% balance is paid after the load is weighed and confirmed in the authorized Recycling Unit.

Privacy Policy

Your Privacy at Circular By Terra

We respect your privacy and protect your personal information with bank-grade standards.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only verified WhatsApp mobile numbers (for secure OTP login and pickup alerts), service address, and pincode to route authorized local collectors.

2. How Your Data is Used

Your address and phone are shared only with the assigned verified collector during active pickup time windows. We never sell or rent your personal information to third-party advertisers.

3. Data Security & Control

All authentication tokens and communication channels are encrypted. You can request deletion of your account and personal records anytime from Settings.
